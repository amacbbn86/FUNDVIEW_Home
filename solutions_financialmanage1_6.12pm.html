Overview

FUNDVIEW Financial Management connects every aspect of your organization’s financial operations into one fully integrated platform. Budgets, purchasing, accounts payable, bank reconciliation, assets, cash receipting, and financial reporting all work together in real time, eliminating duplicate entry and giving finance teams immediate visibility into the information they rely on every day.

One connected platform

Every module shares the same data, so an entry made once is reflected everywhere it matters — eliminating duplicate data entry across departments.

Real-time visibility

A Real-Time General Ledger gives finance teams immediate visibility into revenues, expenditures, and available balances.

Built for local government

Purpose-built for local government accounting, with built-in compliance and audit readiness and U.S.-based support.
